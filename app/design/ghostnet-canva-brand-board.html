Key Screens

UI / UX Moves

Command Shell

  • Sidebar becomes a mission rail with richer wayfinding and section context.
  • Top bar shifts from plain header to operational status line.
  • Background grid supports the sonar identity without going neon.

Survey Theater

  • 3D replay earns more visual space and a dedicated section title.
  • Surrounding panels explain class mix, trend, and latest alert.
  • Quick actions stay near the active mission instead of being scattered.

Intel Feed

  • Recent detections move into a cleaner evidence table with better hover states.
  • Priority badges stay crisp and round for faster scanning.
  • Confidence and timestamps use monospace or tabular treatment for consistency.
